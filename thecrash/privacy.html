The Crash does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), location data, or device identifiers. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cloud tracking tools within the application.

2. Local Device Storage (Save Data)

To allow you to resume your progress, the game stores non-personal gameplay data locally on your device's internal storage, including:

This data never leaves your device and is completely removed if you uninstall the game or clear the application's local storage.
